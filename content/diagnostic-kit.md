<h2>Three routes for this failure</h2>
<div class="product-grid"><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">A</p><h3>The folder is missing from the new profile</h3><p>Desktop, Documents, or Pictures is absent or empty on the new PC. locate the real source folder before creating a new empty one.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">B</p><h3>The files exist but cannot be opened</h3><p>Folders appear but access is denied, or files are owned by the old account. fix inherited permissions on one folder without taking over the whole drive.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">C</p><h3>AppData or application settings are missing</h3><p>The app opens with a blank profile or cannot find its data. restore the application data path without overwriting the new installation.</p></div></div>
